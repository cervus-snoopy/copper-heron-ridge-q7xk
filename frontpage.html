WAR • LEAD STORY — KYIV, UKRAINE

Russia hits three Kyiv bridges in huge overnight barrage; at least 9 dead

1. More than 400 drones and 110 missiles, 27 of them ballistic, struck Ukraine overnight on 20 Nov. Three bridges over the Dnipro — Podilsko-Voskresenskyi, Darnytskyi and Pivdennyi — were hit, cutting power, water and heating in Kyiv and 11 regions. 2. A strike on Kryvyi Rih killed 2 and injured 42, including 4 children. 3. Two Russian Kh-101 missiles and a drone fell in Poland during the attack. 4. Ukraine deployed an experimental laser air-defence system in Kyiv. 5. UNICEF: 1,144 children killed or injured in Ukraine in Jan–Oct 2026, the most in three years.
